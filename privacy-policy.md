# Privacy Policy

aphasia is designed to keep your data on your device.

- **On-device processing.** Speech recognition and reading aloud run entirely on your iPhone. Your audio and transcriptions are not sent to us or any third party.
- **What's stored.** Transcriptions you record are saved locally on your device so you can see your history. You can delete any recording, or clear them all, at any time in Settings.
- **No accounts, no tracking.** We don't require an account, and we don't collect analytics, advertising, or personal information.
- **No audio is kept.** Recordings are transcribed to text; the raw audio is not saved.

Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device.
