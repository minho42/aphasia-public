# Terms of Use

- **What aphasia is.** A communication aid that transcribes speech to text on your device and can read text back aloud.
- **Not medical advice.** aphasia is not a medical device and does not provide medical, legal, or professional advice. Do not rely on it for emergencies or critical decisions.
- **Accuracy.** Speech recognition can make mistakes. Always confirm important information.
