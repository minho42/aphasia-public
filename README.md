# aphasia

A communication aid for people who can't speak easily — aphasia, ICU patients (intubated), ALS (amyotrophic lateral sclerosis), or temporary voice loss.

Tap **Record** when someone speaks to you. The app turns their speech into text, then suggests large, tappable answers so you can reply in a couple of taps.

## What it does

- **Record & transcribe** speech to text, on-device (Whisper).
- **Suggest answers** — AI offers a few tappable replies based on what was said.
- **Read aloud** — plays text back with an on-device voice.
- **History** — keeps a list of past recordings.

## Privacy

Speech recognition and reading run entirely **on-device**.
